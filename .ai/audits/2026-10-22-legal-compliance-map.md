- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Code audited:** `origin/QA1` at `1f03acac7` (read-only). Branch `audit-legal-compliance-2026-10-22`. No live access; no code changed.

## The 10 biggest gaps, ranked by legal risk

1. **No suspicious / cash / terrorist-property reporting registers (FICA-12, FICA-13, FICA-14).** The only trace is a reviewer tick box that triggers nothing. No escalation to the Compliance Officer, no register, no deadline tracking, no filing record. Statutory reporting duties with criminal penalties.
2. **Data-subject requests are not handled (POPIA-9).** No intake, register, deadline, identity check or per-person export or erasure, although the public privacy page promises these rights. Flagged in the June 2026 audit and still open.
3. **Direct-marketing opt-out holes (POPIA-7c, POPIA-7e, POPIA-CPA-1, POPIA-5).** Presentation / CMA emails skip the suppression check and carry no unsubscribe link. The agency unsubscribe footer is not appended to any outgoing mail. The NCC registry cleansing is not built (policy still marked "pending"). Consent model is opt-out by default.
4. **Commission is not blocked without a valid FFC, and the FFC statement is missing from adverts and from the mandate documents in use (PPA-1, PPA-2).** Only the auction publish check looks at FFC. Portal adverts and social images carry no FFC / PPRA statement. The one real FFC-validity clause is not in a live template.
5. **Mandatory Disclosure Form sequence is not enforced (PPA-4).** CoreX only checks that a disclosure document exists before a sale listing goes live. It does not check it was signed before the mandate was accepted, and nothing records the buyer receiving it before an offer. Rentals are exempted in config.
6. **Marital regime and spousal consent are not captured (MPA-1, MPA-2).** Nothing stores in/out of community of property, prompts for a spouse's consent or blocks an offer or mandate without it. Adding a spouse as a signer is manual.
7. **Screening is narrower than it looks (FICA-7, FICA-8, FICA-9).** Sanctions screening is real (nightly FIC list, exact ID hit locks the file) but covers only whoever completed the form: beneficial owners, trustees and directors are never screened, existing clients are not re-screened when the list changes, and a stale list or name-only match never blocks. PEP is self-declaration plus a manual tick, with no list. Staff screening is tick boxes only.
8. **Rental deposit refund deadlines, receipts and tribunal information are absent (RHA-6, RHA-7, RHA-9).** Nothing computes or alerts on the 7 / 14 / 21-day refund periods; the lease says "as soon as possible". No tenant payment receipts (the lease makes receipts request-only). No mention of the Rental Housing Tribunal anywhere.
9. **No security-compromise (breach) process, and the privacy page understates data leaving South Africa (POPIA-11, POPIA-13).** Only a 48-hour contractual promise; no incident register or notification tracker. The privacy page says only Ellie voice text goes to Anthropic, but images and documents are also sent. No PAIA manual and no Regulator-registration field for the Information Officer (POPIA-1b, POPIA-2).
10. **Record-keeping can lose evidence inside 5 years (FICA-11, PPA-5).** One super-admin "destroy all contacts" action hard-deletes every contact's FICA submissions. Published social images are removed after 30 days and portal ad payloads after 45 days, so there is no five-year record of what was actually advertised. Contact purge does not reach linked FICA or signed documents.

Also notable just outside the top 10: no 24-month term check on leases or mandates (CPA-1); no cooling-off period for sales (AoLA-4) and the lease cooling-off wording is static (CPA-4); no tenant-holdover / court-order handling (PIE-2); ID numbers are stored unencrypted (POPIA-15); no FFC / mentor / exam tracking for candidate practitioners beyond take-on ticks (PPA-9); the Directive 12 RMCP submission is not recorded (FICA-17).

## Notes and caveats for whoever uses this

- **QA1 database looks incomplete.** With the QA1 app credentials, tables for RMCP, sanctions lists, training and the compliance return (FICA section) and `properties` / `signature_*` (sales section) returned "doesn't exist" on `corex_qa1`, although their migrations are recorded as run. Findings on those areas were verified by reading the code, and no counts were taken. Someone who owns the QA1 database should check whether QA1 is genuinely missing those tables. Also: 25 sanctions screenings on QA1 show "list stale", which suggests the nightly list fetch is not running there.
- **Per-practitioner FFC counts were not taken:** the `users` table was not readable from the query account.
- **Sales e-signing wording.** Although e-signing of sales documents was out of scope, the sales-side audit saw that an admin can switch e-signing on for offer-to-purchase, sale-agreement and deed templates after ticking a warning (`.ai/specs/ESIGN-CANON.md` §7, 8 Oct ruling). All five such templates on QA1 are currently off. A sales line saying "CoreX does not e-sign sales agreements" should carry that qualifier, or Johan should confirm the wording.
- **FICA is warn-not-block on deals by design** (Johan's ruling of 8 Oct 2026); hard stops are the outside signer's page, marketing a property, an opt-in rentals setting and an exact sanctions hit.
- Offer-to-purchase and mandate wording on QA1 is largely agency-uploaded PDF text, so what it says about marital status, cooling-off, voetstoots or levies is the agency's, not CoreX's, and was not checked.
- The Alienation of Land s29A scope (5 days, up to R250 000) was taken from the brief and not independently verified.
- One live rental mandate template hardcodes "Home Finders Coastal (Agent)": a multi-agency issue outside this audit's scope (PPA-2 note).
