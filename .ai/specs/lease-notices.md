# Lease notices - notice received, agent review, send to the other party

**Status:** built on QA1, 14 Oct 2026 (written BEFORE the code; **re-scoped the same day** - see section 6). Sits beside `leases.md` (notice terms §18, tenancy log §12.10), `rental-renewals.md` §14-19 (what accepting a notice does to the lease and the property) and `rental-portal-access.md` §8 (notice templates and the portal documents list).

## 1. Johan's ruling (14 Oct 2026, verbatim)

"tenant or landlord gives notice -> flows to the agent -> agent has to first work with it -> then can distribute to the other party. Agent must always ensure notices are legal in terms of law and the contract."

So a notice is a **case with a life**, not a one-click record:

`received` -> the agent reviews it (the contract's terms and the law's rules beside it, with flags) -> `accepted` (as given, or with a corrected date) or `rejected` (with a reason) -> **acceptance is what tells the other party**: CoreX mails them straight away from the accepting agent's own mailbox (the dates only) and shows the notice in their portal. There is no template and no separate "send to the other party" step (see §6, which re-scoped this the same day). **Nothing reaches the other party before the agent accepts.** The lease's move-out date is set **only on acceptance** (it then feeds the existing lease-end date logic).

## 2. Why this exists (the faults found on lease 115, 14 Oct)

- "Tenant gave notice" on the lease screen recorded the notice at once and told nobody (`LeaseRenewalService::recordNotice()`, `LeaseRenewalController::recordNotice()`): no mail, no alert, no legal check, move-out date set before anyone reviewed it.
- "Send notice" (`corex.leases.notices.create`, `RentalNoticeController::create()`) dead-ended on "No active notice templates yet": templates were never seeded (`rental_notice_templates` is agency content that nobody had created).
- Neither the tenant nor the owner could give notice from the portal at all.

## 3. Data model

**`lease_notices`** (new; one row per notice given; agency-scoped with `BelongsToAgency`; soft delete only):
`agency_id`, `branch_id` (the lease's), `lease_id`, `given_by` (`tenant` | `landlord`), `via` (`portal` | `agent`), `given_by_contact_id` (the portal person, nullable), `recorded_by_user_id` (the agent who recorded it on their behalf, nullable), `notice_date` (date given), `intended_end_date` (the move-out / end date as given), `reason` (text, nullable), `attachment_path/name/mime/size` (private `local` disk, optional), `status` (`received` | `accepted` | `rejected` | `withdrawn`), `accepted_end_date`, `decision_kind` (`as_given` | `corrected` | `rejected`), `decision_note`, `decided_by_user_id`, `decided_at`, `property_outcome` + `show_available_from_on_portals` (the three-way choice the existing notice dialog asked, Johan 5 Oct 2026 - now asked at acceptance), `flags` (json snapshot of what the review showed when the agent decided), `other_party_told_at`, timestamps, `deleted_at`.
**`lease_settings`** gains `cpa_early_cancel_business_days` (nullable = the default 20). (The breach register's table and columns are in `rental-notices.md`.)
Audit trail: every step writes a `lease_events` row (`notice_received`, `notice_decided`, `notice_sent`) on the lease's tenancy log, with who and when; accepting also writes the existing `notice_recorded` row (it IS the existing `recordNotice()`).

## 4. Pillars and events

Property (the lease's property; the outcome choice re-advertises / withdraws / leaves it - existing rules), Contact (who gave it; the other party), Deal/lease (the notice and move-out date), Agent (the two lease agents are told: `LeaseAgentService::recipientsForEvent()`, the actor excluded). Notification event `lease_notice.received` ("Notice received - needs review") registered in `notification_event_types`; fired in-app per agent (the normal alert popup) plus a direct email each (same pattern as `RentalOwnerHoldService::tellAgents`).

## 5. Flow, step by step

1. **Give notice.** (a) Portal: tenant or owner, "Notices" tab -> "Give notice": lease (when more than one), date given (default today), intended move-out / end date, reason, optional attachment (PDF / image / Word, 10 MB). (b) Agent, on the lease screen: the existing **Tenant gave notice** / **Landlord not renewing** menu items now open a dialog with the same four fields and create a notice **on their behalf** (`via = agent`); they are taken straight to its review. Nothing is sent to anyone else.
2. **Agent is told.** In-app alert popup + email to both lease agents (the recording agent excluded); a **"Notice received - needs review"** tile on the Rentals Command Centre and a row in its needs-action queue; the lease screen shows a **Notice panel** (status, who, dates, link to the review).
3. **Review screen** (`/corex/lease-notices/{id}`): the notice on the left; the **lease's notice terms** on the right (term and end date, month-to-month, notice period, earliest date notice may be given, early cancellation allowed / notice / penalty, whether the terms were confirmed against the signed lease, the agency's CPA business-day setting). **Flags** (computed, never blocking by themselves):
   - *Notice period shorter than the contract allows* (given date + the lease's notice period is later than the intended end date) - names the earliest end date the contract allows and offers it as the corrected date;
   - *Notice given too early* (before the lease's earliest notice date);
   - *CPA early cancellation* (tenant cancelling before a fixed term ends): the Consumer Protection Act lets a tenant cancel a fixed-term lease on **20 business days'** written notice (the agency's setting, default 20); flagged with the earliest end date that satisfies it, any contract early-cancellation notice that is longer, and the contract's penalty; if the contract disallows early cancellation that is flagged too;
   - *Landlord ending a fixed term early* (not allowed without breach);
   - *Wrong date* (intended end in the past, before the date given, given date in the future, before the lease started, or more than two years away);
   - *Terms not confirmed* (the lease's notice terms are agency defaults, not read off the signed lease).
   The agent decides: **Accept** (the move-out date prefilled with the date given; change it = "accepted with corrected date", a note is required; choose what happens to the property, as before), or **Reject** (a reason, required). **Accepting while any warning flag is raised needs a note** saying why it is fine - the agent must always be able to show the notice is lawful and in line with the contract. Rejecting emails and portal-notifies the person who gave it, with the reason (the other party is told nothing).
4. **Accept** runs `LeaseRenewalService::recordNotice()` (sets `notice_date`, `notice_given_by`, `move_out_date`, `notice_outcome`, applies the property outcome, logs `notice_recorded`) and marks the case `accepted`. A lease with an active accepted notice must have it reversed first (the existing **Reverse notice**, which now also marks the case `withdrawn`).
5. **The other party is told on acceptance** (`LeaseNoticeService::tellOtherParty`): an agency-branded email **from the accepting agent's own mailbox** (`RentalMailDispatcher`, `LeaseNoticeAcceptedMail`) to each contact of the other side that has an email - the dates only, never the reason given - and the notice appears in their portal **Notices** tab ("Your landlord gave notice on ... The lease ends on ..."). Logged on the lease. A rejected notice tells the giver, never the other party.

## 6. Re-scope (Johan, 14 Oct 2026) - NO seeded templates

Notice templates are **agency documents** (they relate to the agency's own lease agreement); CoreX seeds none and no longer sends the notice from a template here. Acceptance tells the other party with a plain mail (above). Breach notices and notices to vacate - the agency's own uploaded templates - are `rental-notices.md`. The older **Send notice** screen (`corex.leases.notices.create`) answers "No active notice templates yet" with a real empty state that links to Settings -> Rental Notice Templates, where the agency uploads its own (Word document with placeholders - `rental-notices.md` §3). Setup Wizard: the one setting that came with notices (`cpa_early_cancel_business_days`) is in the Leases step; template documents are agency content on their own screen and are not wizard controls (recorded in `agency-onboarding-setup.md` §5.1).

## 7. Settings (per agency, with defaults)

Notice period default: existing `tenant_notice_period_days` + `_unit` (default 30 days). Early-cancellation defaults: existing `default_early_cancellation_*`. **New: `cpa_early_cancel_business_days`** (default 20; the number of business days' notice the law gives a tenant to cancel a fixed term; weekends and South African public holidays do not count). On Settings -> Leases and in the Setup Wizard's Leases step (`LeaseSettingsController@update`, `has()`-guarded). The notice terms of each lease keep their own screen and permission (`lease_notice_terms.edit`).

## 8. Screens (design standard: CRUD, search, sort, filter, pagination, empty state, scoping)

- **Lease notices list** `/corex/lease-notices` (nav: Rentals -> Notices received). Search: tenant / owner name, property address, reason. Sort: date given, intended end date, status, property (default newest first). Filter: status (default "needs review"), given by, date range, archived. Pagination 25. Real empty state. Visibility: own / branch / agency through `Lease::visibleTo()` - a notice is exactly as visible as its lease; direct URL by id is 403 for anyone who cannot see the lease.
- **Review / case screen** (read, decide, edit while `received`, archive and restore). Equal-size, aligned buttons.
- **Create:** the dialogs on the lease screen (agent) and the portal form. **Update:** the agent may correct a `received` notice's dates / reason (logged). **Archive / restore:** soft delete only, logged; an archived case is out of the list unless the archived filter is on.
- **Portal:** a **Notices** tab for the tenant and for the owner: give notice, and the person's own notices with their status ("with your agent", "accepted - move-out date ...", "not accepted - reason ..."); an owner sees only leases on their own properties, a tenant only their own lease; the final notices sent to them are in **Documents**.
- **Permissions:** `rental_notices.review` (view, record, decide, archive; added to the roles that can renew leases - and the ONLY permission on every way of recording a notice, see audit fixes below). **`rental_notices.view`** opens the history of formal notices SENT (`rental-notices.md`); **`rental_notices.create`** drafts and sends them. Portal endpoints are scoped by the same portal-person rules as every other portal call (`RentalPortalScopeService`).

## 9. Not changed / reported

- The existing **Send notice** screen (`corex.leases.notices.create`) stays for formal notices that are not a reply to a received notice, using ONLY the agency's own uploaded templates (no seeded or default templates - §6) with the auto-filled placeholders and a preview; with no templates it shows the empty state that links to Settings -> Rental Notice Templates.
- Lease notice terms (`LeaseNoticeTermsService`) are read, never edited, by the review.
- Business days use weekdays less `PublicHoliday` rows for ZA (seeded by the leave module); a year with no holidays seeded counts weekdays only, and the review says so.

## Audit fixes 2026-10-19 (rental application / lease audit, A6 A9 A17 A22 A28)

- **A22 - the spec no longer contradicts itself.** Code behaviour wins: §6's re-scope is the design. What I chose: (1) §1 no longer says the agent "sends the final notice from a template" - acceptance itself mails the other party; (2) §8's permission line no longer lists "send"; (3) §9 no longer says the Send notice screen has default templates (CoreX seeds none); (4) §10's test list no longer has the template/send/default-template tests. §5 steps 4-5 were already right.
- **A6 - an archived case does not block.** `LeaseNoticeService::give()` only counts NON-archived "received" notices from the same side, so archiving a stuck case clears the way for a new one. Every action on an archived case (edit, accept, reject) is refused with "This notice is archived. Restore it before doing anything else with it." - by direct POST as well as in the screen. Restoring a "received" case is refused if a newer one from the same side is already waiting. Archiving an archived case / restoring a live one does nothing and logs nothing.
- **A9 - "told" means told.** The other party is logged as told, and `other_party_told_at` is set, only for contacts an email actually went to. A contact with no email address, or whose email failed, is logged "could NOT be told" (named, with the reason), and when nobody was reached `other_party_told_at` stays empty. The notice screen then says plainly that they were not told, and why to look at the lease log.
- **A17 - one way in.** The lease screen's "Tenant gave notice" / "Landlord not renewing" dialogs, the notice-cases route and the mobile API (`POST /api/v1/leases/{lease}/renewal/tenant-notice|landlord-notice`) all validate through `LeaseNoticeService::validateAgentEntry()` and all need `rental_notices.review` (the dialogs and the Command Centre's "Record notice" link are hidden without it). Before, `leases.renew` alone could record a case it could not open. Migration `2026_11_19_100000` grants `.review` to every role that holds `leases.renew`, so nobody loses the ability. Route names are unchanged.
- **A28 - one date rule.** `LeaseNoticeService::dateRules()` / `dateMessages()`: date given not in the future; move-out date AFTER the date given; not more than two years away. Used by the portal, the agent's dialogs, the notice-cases route, the mobile API and "correct a received notice". Previously only the portal required the move-out date to be after the date given. The history of formal notices sent (`corex.rental-notices.*`) is gated by the new `rental_notices.view` (migration grants it to every role that holds `.create`; also in the default sets of the managerial roles that review notices), no longer by `.create`.
- Tests: `tests/Feature/LeaseNotices/LeaseNoticeAuditFixesTest.php`.

## 11. The agency's EXPIRY NOTICE to the tenant - Consumer Protection Act (AT-489, 21 Oct 2026, cc1)

**Johan's rulings (verbatim in substance):** build CoreX on CPA law. The 40-80 business days in clause 5.1 of the residential lease is the AGENCY'S expiry notice to the tenant (CPA s14(2)(c)), not a termination notice. Counting is CPA s2(6): the first day is excluded, the last day is included, Saturdays, Sundays and South African public holidays do not count. Tenant early cancellation is a separate rule: 20 business days' written notice (s14(2)(b)).

**The law is a constant, the agency chooses the day.** Not more than **80** and not less than **40** business days before a fixed-term lease ends (`LeaseSetting::CPA_EXPIRY_NOTICE_MAX_/MIN_BUSINESS_DAYS`; not settings). Agency settings (Settings -> Leases, the Setup Wizard's Leases step, settings search; audit-logged in `rental_setting_audit`):
- `lease_settings.expiry_notice_send_business_days` - "Send the lease expiry notice ___ business days before the lease ends": **40-80 only** (validated on the screen and on the server; a figure outside it that reaches the table anyway is held to the range when read), **default 60**.
- `lease_settings.expiry_notice_alert_business_days` - "If it has not gone out, alert the lease's agents when ___ business days remain": **between 40 and the send day**, **default 45**. No threshold is hardcoded.
- **Replaces** the old months-based setting (`default_earliest_notice_months`, "months into the lease"): migration `2026_10_21_110000` converts an agency's months to the nearest business days (21.67 a month), holds it to 40-80, writes it as that agency's send day, clears the old value, and audit-logs both steps (source `migration`). The column stays, unused. HFC had none, so it simply gets the default 60. The typed "earliest notice date" is gone from the capture screen and the Notice terms card (nothing is typed for the expiry notice; a renewal no longer carries one forward). Stored values on existing leases, and the lease agreement templates (#88/#92), are untouched - wording goes through Johan's compliance officer first.

**Per lease** (`App\Services\Rentals\LeaseExpiryNoticeService`, `BusinessDayService`): "Expiry notice due between *end - 80 bd* and *end - 40 bd*" and "CoreX sends it on *end - send-day bd*", computed (never typed) on the capture screen (live, in the browser, with the server's holiday list), the lease screen's Notice terms card, and the server. "N business days before the end" is the business day with exactly N business days after it up to and including the end date (when the end date is itself a weekend/holiday it adds nothing to the count). Holidays: the leave module's own calculation (fixed days, Good Friday, Family Day; computed, so an unseeded year is right) joined with the `public_holidays` table; a holiday on a Sunday makes the Monday one, and when that Monday is already a holiday the Tuesday (Christmas Day on a Sunday). A month-to-month lease (no end date), a lease with a notice on file, or a renewed one has no expiry notice.

**Sending** - the agency's own wording: a new template type **Lease expiry notice** (Settings -> Rental Notice Templates; CoreX ships none). Daily `leases:expiry-notices` (06:30; `--dry-run`, `--agency=`, `--lease=`; agencies with Leases off are skipped): on/after the send day, for a running fixed-term lease whose notice has not gone out, it fills the template and emails the tenants **from the lease agent's own mailbox** (the existing notice sender, `RentalNoticeService::send`), then records it on the lease (`leases.expiry_notice_sent_at/via/by`, tenancy-log row `expiry_notice_sent`). A notice an agent sends from the template by hand (Send notice) counts the same; so does **Mark as given** (a note: how it was handed over). No template, or no tenant email: CoreX cannot send, so it alerts instead.

**Alerts and overdue.** When the notice has not gone out and the alert day is reached, the lease's agents get ONE alert (notification type `lease.expiry_notice_due`; tenancy-log row `expiry_notice_alerted`; `leases.expiry_notice_alerted_at`); the daily run keeps retrying to send. Fewer than 40 business days left and it still has not gone out: the lease is **overdue** - a red banner on the lease screen ("Send the expiry notice now" / "Mark as given") and a **"Expiry notice overdue"** row in the Rentals Command Centre needs-action queue (live, not stored). States: not due, due (send day reached), alert, overdue, sent, none.

**Tenant early cancellation (s14(2)(b), 20 business days)** is unchanged and separate: `cpa_early_cancel_business_days` (default 20) in `LeaseNoticeReviewService`, wherever a tenant's notice is recorded (portal, agent dialog, review screen). Its business-day arithmetic now uses the same `BusinessDayService`, so the two rules count identically; the "holidays not loaded" flag is gone because the holidays are computed.

**Tests:** `tests/Feature/Leases/LeaseExpiryNoticeAt489Test.php` (counting across Christmas/New Year and the Sunday-to-Monday rule, settings range + audit + migration conversion, states, the daily send/alert, lease screen, Command Centre, capture screen). Browser proof: the real partial in headless Chromium, the browser's dates compared with the server's.

**Deliberately not built here (flag for Johan):** no tile for "expiry notices overdue" on the Command Centre (the queue row only); the default wording of the notice is the agency's (an agency with no template is alerted, never sent a CoreX-written notice).
