## 21. Sherry walk, 13 Oct 2026 (cc1) - "the lease never completes" is the agent's approval step, and the screens now say so

cc5's full walk reported: after the agent, tenant and landlord signed, the landlord's last page said "All parties have signed!" but the lease stayed Draft / "out for signing" and no "fully signed" email arrived. **Cause: not a fault.** The e-sign engine deliberately holds a clean, fully signed document at `pending_agent_approval` (AT-322, `SignatureService::holdForFinalAgentReview`): filing and the "Fully signed" emails run only inside `completeDocument()`, which only the agent's Review & Approve (`approveAndAdvance`) calls. The lease follows the envelope (`awaiting_agent_review`, chip "Needs my approval", next step "Approve the signed agreement"), and goes `signed` / `active` the moment the agent approves - proved on QA1 lease 106: approval 17:05:52, lease active and both "Fully signed" emails and "Lease agreement signed and active" in Mailpit by 17:06:13. The listener (`UpdateLeaseSigningState`) is synchronous, the QA1 queue holds only thumbnail jobs, and **no scheduler is involved**: `leases:reconcile-signing` (daily 00:20, `routes/console.php`) is only the safety net for a missed announcement; opening the lease page also re-checks (`Lease::reconcileSigning()`), so QA1 (no scheduler) needs nothing to run.

**What was wrong, and is fixed:** (1) the last signer's page (`signatures/external/completed.blade.php`) claimed "All parties have signed!" when nothing had been filed or sent; while the document is held it now says "Everyone has signed." and that the agency now approves it and the copy is emailed once that is done (`data-qa="signed-awaiting-approval"`); (2) the lease screen's locked-fields sentence said "out for signing" for a lease that is signed and waiting - `Lease::lockedMessage()` is state-aware ("Everyone has signed. Approve the signed agreement to finish ...").

**Open question for Johan (business, not built):** should a signed lease with NO changes go out and go live straight away, or always wait for the agent's approval click? For a one-person agency the click is the same person who sent it. Recommendation: straight away when nothing was changed in e-sign, with the approval step kept for any changed or flagged agreement - but that overrides the AT-322 ruling ("nothing files or emails before review"), so it waits for his word.
