## Agreement pre-fill: erf and lessor address (14 Oct 2026, rental re-walk fault 1)
The pre-fill does read both: "Lessor address" is the landlord CONTACT's own address (`LeaseSigningLauncher::addressFor()`), "Erf no" is the PROPERTY's erf. The erf is kept in two columns - `erf_number` (title-deed / SG figure) and `property_number` (the property form's "Property / Erf Number" box) - and the snapshot read only the second; `propertySnapshot()` now takes `erf_number` first, then `property_number`, for `erf`, `erf_no` and `erf_number`. The first agreement (`buildStepData`) and the renewal (`buildFlow`) share the snapshot and recipient rows, so both get it. **Not a bug, but worth knowing:** on the walk's own records the property's erf box was blank and the landlord contact had no residential address, so there was nothing to fill - those two fields stay empty until someone records them (a landlord's address is entered on the contact's Info; the new-contact form on a property does not ask for it). Tests: `tests/Feature/Leases/AgreementErfAndLessorPrefillTest.php`.
