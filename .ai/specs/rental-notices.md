# Rental notices - breach notices, notices to vacate, the breach register

**Status:** built on QA1, 14 Oct 2026 (this document was written BEFORE the code). Johan's brief, 14 Oct 2026. Companion to `lease-notices.md` (a tenant / landlord GIVING notice to end the lease - the review flow) and `rental-portal-access.md` §8 (the older "Send notice" screen and notice templates).

**Rule:** breach notices and notices to vacate are the **agency's own documents** - they relate to the agency's own lease agreement - so CoreX supplies **no template wording**. The agency uploads its own; nothing is seeded.

## 1. What this is

1. **Templates** - the agency uploads three document templates (Word documents with placeholders): *breach notice (payment)*, *breach notice (other)*, *notice to vacate*.
2. **Bulk payment breach** - from the leases list the agent ticks leases and chooses **Breach notice**; a spreadsheet-like screen lists them with an **Outstanding amount** to fill in. Only rows with an amount become a breach.
3. **One-off "other" breach** - the agent picks a lease, who is in breach, the lease clause and the reason.
4. **Notice to vacate** - only on a lease with an issued, unremedied **tenant** breach whose **remedy window has passed**.
5. Every notice is sent from the **agent's own mailbox** (so it is also in their Sent items), the **other party is told**, both parties see the notice in their portal, and it is on the lease's log.
6. A **breach register** lists every breach and where it stands.

## 2. Pillars and events

Contact (tenant, landlord), Property (the lease's), Deal/lease (the breach is a fact about the lease; the lease screen shows breach status and remedy deadline), Agent (the issuing agent sends from their mailbox; the lease agents see the register). Every step writes a `lease_events` row (`breach_issued`, `breach_remedied`, `vacate_issued`, `breach_edited`).

## 3. Templates (Settings -> Rental Notice Templates)

The existing agency templates screen (`RentalNoticeTemplate`, `rental_notice_templates`, agency-scoped, create / edit / archive / restore, search / sort / filter / pagination) gains three **types**: `breach_payment`, `breach_other`, `notice_to_vacate` (the older single `breach` template stays and reads as "other"). **Upload:** the form takes a Word document (`.docx`, up to 10 MB), converts it to the template body with the same converter the e-sign importer uses (Mammoth - `resources/js/mammoth-convert.mjs`), keeps the original file on the template, and the agent can edit the converted text before saving; the body can also still be typed. This is the agency-supplied-document mechanism of the e-sign importer **without** its signature-field detection (a notice is not signed on screen). Placeholders are `{{name}}` and are filled from the lease and the breach: `agency_name agency_address agency_phone agency_email agent_name today property_address tenant_name landlord_name lease_ref lease_start_date lease_end_date monthly_rent amount clause reason breach_date remedy_days remedy_deadline`; anything else is typed by the agent as `name=value` lines. A placeholder still empty at send time **stops the send** and is named. With **no template of a type** the screen that needs it shows a real empty state that links to the templates screen - never a dead end.

## 4. Data model

**`lease_breaches`** (one row per breach; `BelongsToAgency`; soft delete only): `lease_id`, `branch_id`, `kind` (`payment` | `other`), `breach_party` (`tenant` | `landlord`), `amount` (payment), `clause`, `reason`, `status` (`issued` | `remedied` | `escalated`), `issued_on`, `remedy_deadline`, `remedy_business_days` (the window in force when issued), `issued_by_user_id`, `rental_notice_id` (the breach notice sent), `batch_ref` (a bulk send), `remedied_at/by/note`, `vacate_issued_at/by`, `vacate_rental_notice_id`. `rental_notices.lease_breach_id`; `rental_notice_templates.source_file_name/path` (the uploaded Word document). Setting **`lease_settings.breach_remedy_business_days`** (default 20).

## 5. Rules

- **Remedy window** = the agency's `breach_remedy_business_days` (default **20 business days**, the Consumer Protection Act's period for a breach to be put right before a lease can be cancelled for it; weekends and public holidays do not count - `LeaseNoticeReviewService::addBusinessDays`). The deadline = issue date + window. Settings -> Leases and the Setup Wizard's Leases step (`LeaseSettingsController@update`, `has()`-guarded).
- **Payment breach** is against the tenant. **Other breach**: the agent chooses the party (tenant or landlord). **Notice to vacate** is only for a tenant breach, only while it is `issued` (not remedied) and only **after** the remedy deadline; before that the lease screen says "from <date>". Issuing it sets the breach `escalated`.
- **Mark remedied** (agent, with a note) ends the breach (`remedied`); it can no longer be escalated.
- One open (`issued`) payment breach per lease at a time: a second is refused ("a breach notice is already out for this lease") - in a bulk send that lease is skipped and named, the rest go.
- **Sending** (`LeaseBreachService`): fill the agency's template -> PDF -> filed as a Document -> emailed to the party in breach (each contact with an email) **from the issuing agent's own mailbox** through `RentalMailDispatcher` (SMTP + Sent-folder copy; falls back, audited, to the shared mailer when the agent has no mailbox - the existing e-sign behaviour). **The other party is told**: tenant in breach -> the landlord gets "A breach notice has been issued against your tenant X for outstanding R<amount>" (or "...for a breach of the lease"); landlord in breach -> the tenant is told; same mailbox, agency-branded, no CoreX text. Both parties see the notice (breach notice, and the notice to vacate) in their portal under **Documents** (kind *Notice*); the portal **Notices** tab does not carry it.

## 6. Screens (design standard: CRUD, search, sort, filter, pagination, empty state, scoping)

- **Leases list** gains a tick box per row and a **Breach notice** bulk button (permission `rental_breaches.manage`).
- **Bulk breach screen** `/corex/breaches/bulk`: the chosen leases (own / branch / agency scope applies to each id - an id the user cannot see is dropped), columns tenant, property, monthly rent, last paid (not tracked in CoreX yet - shown as a dash), **Outstanding amount** (R, input). Template picker (the agency's active payment-breach templates). Rows with no amount are ignored. **Send** creates one breach per lease with an amount and reports what was sent and what was skipped (and why).
- **One-off breach** `/corex/leases/{lease}/breaches/create`: breach party, lease clause (typed; the agreement's own clauses are not stored as a list in CoreX today, so it is typed - quoted into the notice), reason, template, preview, send.
- **Breach register** `/corex/breaches` (nav: Rentals -> Breach Register): search (tenant / landlord name, property address, clause, reason); sort (issued, remedy deadline, amount, status, property; default newest first); filter (status, kind, who is in breach, issued date range, archived); pagination 25; empty state; own / branch / agency through `Lease::visibleTo()`; direct URL by id blocked. Row -> **breach screen**: the facts, the document(s), who was told, status, **Mark remedied**, **Issue notice to vacate** (only when allowed), archive / restore. Equal-size aligned buttons.
- **Lease screen**: a **Breach panel** (each breach: kind, party, amount, status, remedy deadline, link) and a **Breach notice** button.
- **Permissions:** `rental_breaches.view` (register, breach screen, downloads) and `rental_breaches.manage` (issue, remedy, vacate, archive), added to the roles that can renew leases. Templates stay under `rental_notice_templates.manage_settings`. **Audit fix 2026-10-19 (A28):** the history of formal notices SENT (`corex.rental-notices.index/show/download-document`, the "Notices sent" tab) needs **`rental_notices.view`**, not `rental_notices.create` (drafting and sending); a read-only role can now see what was sent. A migration granted `.view` to every role that held `.create`.

## 7. Not changed / reported

## 7a. The agency's own notice documents (AT-462, 15 Oct 2026)

Built so an agency's real letters can be loaded as templates and print correctly. Everything below is configuration or agency data; CoreX still ships no wording.

- **Letterhead.** Every notice PDF opens with the agency's letterhead read from the agency record - logo (branch logo first), name, address, phones (both), email, company registration, FFC, and VAT number (only when the agency is VAT-registered). A detail the agency has not filled in is left off. Templates therefore start at the date, never with a typed letterhead. Extra placeholders for the body: `agency_phone_secondary agency_reg_no agency_ffc_no agency_vat_no tenant_email breach_summary vacate_date vacate_time`.
- **Clause block.** `rental_notice_templates.default_clause` (editable on the template form) is the clause text the notice quotes at `{{clause}}`. The one-off breach form starts its clause box from the chosen template's block and the agent edits it to quote the clause actually breached; a payment breach (no clause typed) prints the template's block as saved. Placeholder values keep their line breaks.
- **Remedy window basis.** `lease_settings.breach_remedy_day_basis` = `business` (default; weekends and public holidays skipped) or `calendar` (a lease that says "within 7 days of written notice"). `lease_breaches.remedy_day_basis` remembers which it was issued under; screens say "7 calendar days" / "20 business days". Settings -> Leases and the Setup Wizard's Leases step (same `has()`-guarded saver).
- **Notice to vacate captures its date and time.** The form asks the date the tenant must be out by (required, not in the past) and the time (starts from `lease_settings.vacate_time`, default 12:00, also in settings + wizard), and shows the breach notices on file (`breach_summary`: date - reason, tenant breaches not remedied) in an editable box. Stored on `lease_breaches.vacate_date / vacate_time`; printed as "30 November 2026" and "12h00". The gate: tenant breach, still open, the remedy-by day has gone by - from the day after the remedy-by date (section 7b, 20 Oct 2026 ruling).
- **Where a notice shows.** The lease screen's Breach panel links the breach notice and notice to vacate PDFs; both are Documents on the lease, entries in the tenancy log, and Documents (kind Notice) in both portals. (The lease screen has no separate Documents tab.)

## 7b. Johan's rulings, 15 Oct 2026 (AT-462 round 2)

1. **Breach templates per agency.** An agency makes as many breach notice templates as it wants (Settings -> Rental Notice Templates: full CRUD, soft delete, agency-scoped, settings-search entry). A template = name, letter body with merge fields, default clause block, and its **own remedy period in days** (`rental_notice_templates.remedy_days`; blank = the agency's "Days a tenant has to put a breach right" setting, counted in business or calendar days per the agency's basis setting). Two are loaded **as HFC's data** (CoreX ships none): "Payment breach" (keeps the NCR sentence and the "as per the attached statement" line, with the amount) and "Ad hoc breach" (the agent quotes clause + reason at send time; no NCR sentence). The breach create screen lists every breach template: a **payment** template (type `breach_payment`) asks for the amount outstanding (always the tenant; the template's clause block is the clause, editable); an **ad hoc** one (`breach_other`) asks for who is in breach, the clause and the reason. The bulk payment sheet uses payment templates only. The remedy-by date = the day it is sent + the template's days.
2. **Follow-up when the remedy-by date is reached.** When today is the remedy-by date (or later) and the breach is still `issued`: (a) the lease's agents get ONE alert (`lease_breach.remedy_date_reached`, its own alert type, "Breach remedy date reached - was it fixed? - <address>") from the daily command `breaches:check-remedy-dates` (06:15; `--dry-run`, `--lease=`; `lease_breaches.remedy_alert_sent_at` makes it once); (b) the lease screen's **Next bar** shows "Breach remedy date reached — was it fixed?" (ahead of every other next step, live, whether or not the command has run) with **Breach remedied** (date put right - not future, not before the notice - plus a note; closes the breach, back to the lease) and **Send notice to vacate** (opens the breach screen at the vacate form). People without `rental_breaches.manage` see the question only. The **notice-to-vacate gate** stays: tenant breach, still unremedied - but see the 20 Oct 2026 ruling below: it now opens the day AFTER the remedy-by date.
3. **Notice-to-vacate period.** Agency setting "Days for notice to vacate" (`lease_settings.vacate_notice_days`, default 30; Settings -> Leases + wizard): the vacate date starts as the day it is sent + that many days, editable on the notice; the time-of-day setting stays.
4. **Proof-of-payment address.** Agency setting "Rental payment proof email" (`lease_settings.payment_proof_email`; Settings -> Leases + wizard + settings search; blank = the agency's own email), merged as `{{payment_proof_email}}` - never a constant in a template or in code.
5. **Breach Register status tiles** (`/corex/breaches`) are rendered by the SHARED rentals list component `<x-rental-list-controls>` (the one Leases, Inspections, Fault Reports and Work Orders use - same markup, size, count styling, active highlight, a Total tile first, per-page and Show archived controls); they are never restyled by hand. Tiles: Open (issued, remedy-by date in the future) / Remedy date reached (issued, date reached) / Remedied / Notice to vacate sent (escalated). Click filters, click the active tile again clears; each count is the same query as the list (own / branch / agency scope per Role Manager, plus the search, kind / party / date / archived filters) with the tile's own condition, so it equals the rows shown; search, sort and pagination keep the tile. The old Status dropdown is replaced by the tiles (a legacy `?status=` link still filters). There is no separate "vacated / closed" state in the data (the breach is `escalated` once the notice to vacate is sent), so there is no tile for it.

## 7c. Audit fixes 2026-10-20 (notices / portal audit)

1. **Notice to vacate - the tenant has the whole last remedy day (finding 1).** Ruling (paraphrase, not a quote): **Johan, 20 Oct 2026: the tenant has the whole last remedy day; the notice to vacate is available from the next day.** This replaces the 15 Oct "from the remedy-by date itself" wording in 7b item 2. `LeaseBreach::remedyWindowPassed()` is true only when today (in the agency's timezone - `Agency::outreachTimezone()`, the app timezone until agencies carry their own) is LATER than `remedy_deadline`; `canIssueVacate()` and `LeaseBreachService::issueVacate()` use it; `vacateAvailableFrom()` is the day after. The "was it fixed?" follow-up is unchanged and still asked ON the remedy-by date (`remedyDateReached()`: the 06:15 alert, the Next bar question, the "date reached - was it fixed?" line), but on that day the Next bar offers only "Breach remedied" - "Send notice to vacate" appears the next day. The breach screen's "Not yet available" line and the refusal read: "The tenant has the whole of <date> to put this right. A notice to vacate is available from <next day>."
2. **One vacate date (finding 7).** The date and time on the vacate form are the only source: a typed `vacate_date=` / `vacate_time=` line in the extra figures is ignored (controller and `issueVacate`), so the printed notice and `lease_breaches.vacate_date / vacate_time` always agree.
3. **Notices menu and hub gates match the tabs (finding 8).** The "Notices" item shows exactly when `NoticesTabs::forUser()` has a tab; the hub route and the outer Rentals group gate use `NoticesTabs::permissions()` (so `rental_notices.create` alone no longer shows an item that answers 403, and `rental_notice_templates.manage_settings` alone still shows the Rentals menu).
4. **Template HTML is cleaned (note).** CoreX has no general HTML purifier package (none added). `App\Support\Html\NoticeHtmlCleaner` removes scripts, frames / objects, forms, SVG / MathML, event-handler attributes, `javascript:` / `vbscript:` / non-image `data:` addresses and script-bearing `style` values, keeping the letter markup; it runs when a template is saved (typed or from a Word document) and again when a notice is rendered (`RentalNoticeService::render`), so templates saved earlier are covered on screen and in the PDF. Placeholder values stay escaped. The rules mirror what e-sign template import already refuses (`TemplateContentSafety`).
5. **Notice reverse / change-outcome (finding 5)** need `rental_notices.review` - see `lease-notices.md`.
6. Tests: `LeaseBreachFlowTest`, `BreachTemplatesAndFollowUpTest` (vacate gate), `AgencyNoticeDocumentsTest` (one vacate date), `NoticesHubTest` (gates), `NoticesPortalAuditFixesTest` (cleaner).
