## 1. Business rules (plain words)

> **Amended 26 Oct 2026 (Johan, ruling 6 in §11) — "Mode 2" and "Mode 3" are no longer separate choices.** They live on the SAME document and are one choice, "Adopt once". Wherever this spec (or older code comments / tests) says Mode 2 it means *click a place to apply the adopted mark*; Mode 3 means *press "Apply to all my places"*; an "Adopt once" signer can do either or both. Stored values `adopt_then_stamp` and `adopt_and_fill_all` are retired and mean `adopt_once` (a data migration moved agencies and envelopes; code also maps the old values on read). §4's step lists still describe the two behaviours accurately.

1. **Each agency has one setting, "How my signers sign", with two choices.** It is set by someone with the e-sign settings permission. **Default is "Sign and initial each place"** — an agency that never opens the screen sees no change at all.
2. **(a) Sign and initial each place (today, HFC).** The signer opens the document and signs or initials each place on the document, one by one. Nothing about this choice changes.
3. **(b) Adopt once.** When the signer opens the document they first **adopt** their signature and their initial once (draw or type). A short agreement is shown: *"By signing here you agree this is your signature and initial and that it will be applied wherever you click to sign or initial on this document."* After that the signer can **click each signature place and initial place** to apply the adopted mark there (one click = one place, recorded separately) **and/or press "Apply to all my places"**: CoreX applies the adopted mark to **every signature and initial place that belongs to that signer** on the document, after a confirmation that says exactly how many places will be filled (the "Apply to all" confirmation also carries the second approved sentence of 23 Oct: *"…applied to every signature and initial place on this document that is yours, without you clicking each one."*). **A click on any place, or "Apply to all", with no adoption on record (or an adoption missing either mark) opens the adopt popup first and then applies what was asked — never a red block, never a silent failure; the server refuses with 422 `adoption_required` and nothing is written.** An existing adoption is reused; "Change my marks" stays available. The popup shows on first open of the document (after the signer chooses to sign electronically; never over the method chooser).
4. **The signing screen header is sticky** (locked at the top while scrolling) and carries: the signing model in plain words ("Sign each place" / "Adopt once, click to apply or apply to all"), live progress ("x of N places done"), the primary actions (Adopt / Change my marks, Apply to all my places when adopting once, Finish) and the one thing blocking Finish with a link to it. Compact on a phone. (Johan's screen rule: returned + next action first, no decorative space.)
5. **The mode used for a document is fixed when it is sent.** If the agency changes the setting later, documents already out for signing finish in the mode they were sent in. Nobody is switched half-way through a signature.
6. **The mode applies to the people who sign by link (tenants, landlords, sellers, buyers, directors, witnesses, supervisors).** The agent keeps the agent's own way of working (saved signature unlocked with a PIN, and apply-to-all — already built and already permitted for agents). (Ruling 3, §11.)
7. **Some places are never filled automatically, in any mode.** These are the places where the law or Johan's rule needs the person to act deliberately, one at a time:
   - per-clause condition initials and "every co-tenant must initial this" clauses;
   - initials that accept an amendment or a changed disclosure answer (AT-303);
   - the YES / NO / N/A answers in a disclosure grid (they are answers, not signatures);
   - anything the template marks "individual consent required".
   When adopting once, the signer may place their adopted initial on these with a deliberate click (that click is the deliberate act); they are always **excluded from "Apply to all"** and the signer is told how many remain.
8. **Every place that gets a mark has its own record: who, which place, when, from where, by which method** (drew it there, clicked to stamp, or applied automatically after the one confirmation). The adoption itself is recorded with the exact agreement wording and its version.
9. **Nothing is ever overwritten.** Changing a mark creates a new generation; the original stays on record (§5).
10. **The signed document and certificate look the same to the reader as today**, with the certificate gaining the adoption line and the per-place method.

## 2. What the system does today (investigation findings)

**Signing views.** Recipient: `resources/views/docuperfect/signatures/external/sign.blade.php` (the only recipient view; web/CDS places are inline `data-sig-id` blocks, initials are page boxes injected client-side by `partials/a4-page-styles.blade.php` — one per signing party per page except each document's last page; PDF-marker documents post each place to `/sign/{token}/capture/{marker}`). Agent: `resources/views/docuperfect/signatures/sign.blade.php` — **separate copy of the JS**, served by `SignatureController`, posts to `webSignComplete`. Only `a4-page-styles`, `disclosure-logic` and `_change-initial-affordance` are shared.

**Capture UI.** One shared modal `partials/_capture-modal.blade.php` (Draw via SignaturePad, Type in Dancing Script cropped to ink, and "Use my saved signature" for agents only). Mobile: `touch-action:none` on the canvas, fixed 160px pad, no orientation handling.

**Anything like "adopt once" already?**
- **Agents:** `agent_signatures` (signature image, initial image, PIN hash) + `AgentSignatureService` (8h unlock per document) + "Apply to remaining / Apply to all pages" prompts. Gated on `isAgent`.
- **Recipients: deliberately none.** Code comments (Phase 1B.9, FIX 2): "Recipients must initial each page individually for legal informed-consent reasons".
- **Doctrine** `esign-ceremony-v3.md` §2 already says every signer *adopts once at entry*; client parties then **gate per surface** (each placement a timestamped consent event, "silent apply-all forbidden"); agents apply-all. **Mode 2 is exactly that doctrine; Mode 3 reverses it for clients** (accepted by Johan as an agency choice, ruling 1).
- Nearest working model: Platform E-Sign agreement (`platform-esign/agreement/_js.blade.php`) — initials typed once, one tap per page, stored in `platform_esign_initials`; "copy Part A signature" button.
- Every other signature capture (FICA form, inspection sign-by-link and "Sign now", inventory, rental application, title deeds, policy acks) is its own pad and reuses nothing.

**What is stored per place today.**
- PDF-marker path: `signatures` row per marker (image base64, signer name/email, IP, user agent, `signed_at`, drawn/typed). No generation/superseded columns. External `capture()` has no already-signed check so a re-capture appends a second row (the PDF reads the oldest); the agent route returns 409.
- **Web/CDS path (the path CDS leases/mandates use): per-place images live only in the JSON `documents.web_template_data` (`signatures[blockId]`, `signed_initials[partyKey]`) — no per-place timestamp, IP or user agent.** Time/IP/UA exist only per signer on `signature_requests` and in one `web_signing_completed` audit row (`{party_role, field_count, signature_count, disclosure_count}`).
- `condition_initials` (per-condition, immutable: `save()` throws if the row exists); `section_acceptances` (`updateOrCreate` — can overwrite); `amendment_acceptances.initial_image`.

**Stamping.** `SigningController::completeWeb` → `CanonicalInkComposer::bakeInk` bakes images into `canonical_html` by `data-recipient-identity` → `DocumentSealService::seal` writes an append-only, hash-chained `document_sealed_versions` row per hop → `SignaturePdfService` renders the PDF (`injectInitialsPagination` restores per-page initials). Certificate: `SignatureController::downloadCertificate` → `audit-certificate.blade.php` (document hash; per party name, email, method, completed_at, IP, counts; audit trail; legal statement). It prints **no** per-place times and **no** consent wording.

**Server gate today.** `completeWeb` checks consent, "at least one captured mark", condition initials and amendment initials. **It does not check that every place was signed — exact counts are client-gated.** It also does not validate image size/format.

**Consent today.** Checkbox text ("…consent to signing this document electronically … ECTA Section 13") is not stored — only a fixed string + `consent_timestamp` in the `electronic_consent_given` audit row. The gateway consent (exact 5-point text) is stored in `esign_consent_log.consent_text`. **No consent wording has a version number anywhere** except Platform E-Sign (`platform_esign_wording_versions`) and the agent-side `template_esign_acknowledgements.wording_version`.

**Evidence/generations rule — does it hold?** Johan's "a re-signature never overwrites the original" is **not written verbatim in any e-sign spec** (`ESIGN-CANON.md` has no evidence section) and is only partly enforced: it holds for `DocumentSealedVersion`, `SignatureAuditLog`, `ConditionInitial`, and — as an explicit `superseded_at / superseded_by_*` pattern — for rental inspection and inventory signatures (`RentalInspectionSignature.php`, "never edited in place"). It does **not** hold for the web-path JSON (a re-sign of the same block id overwrites the value; only the sealed versions keep history) or for `section_acceptances`. **Modes 2/3 therefore must not rely on the JSON; they get their own append-only per-place table (§5).**

> **Update 23 Oct 2026 (conductor/Johan): the web-path overwrite below was ruled a BUG against Johan's standing signature-evidence rule (9 Sep 2026) and is being fixed first, on its own branch (`esign-evidence-generations-2026-10-23`, QA1) — see `ESIGN-CANON.md` §10. That fix adds `signature_place_generations` (per place: generation, time, IP, user agent, latest renders) and `section_acceptances.generation`. Modes 2 and 3 should REUSE that table for their per-place records rather than the separate `signature_placements` table sketched in §5 (add `method` and `adoption_id` columns to it); §5 and build step 2 are to be re-cut accordingly once the fix has landed.**

## 4. The three flows, step by step

**Mode 1** — no code path changes. (Regression-protected by tests, §9.)

**Mode 2 — adopt, then click to stamp** (recipient view only)
1. Signer opens the link; existing identity/gateway consent unchanged.
2. **Adoption step** (before any place is clickable): the shared capture modal collects a signature (draw/type) and an initial (draw/type; typed defaults to the name's initials as Platform E-Sign does). The agreement text (§6) sits above a tick box. "Adopt" persists a `signature_adoptions` row (§5) and turns the places into one-click stamps.
3. Every signature/initial place shows as "click to sign / initial". A click places the adopted image immediately (no modal), writes a placement (§5) with the server's time, and moves the bottom-bar "N items remaining → Go to next" (`scrollToNextIncomplete`, `external/sign.blade.php`) on. Consent (existing ECTA checkbox) and the completion gate work as today.
4. A signer who dislikes their adopted mark can re-adopt: a new adoption generation is created; marks already placed keep the old adoption; the signer can remove and re-click a place (new placement generation, old one superseded not deleted).
5. Complete → `completeWeb` as today, plus the stamped-image check (§7).

**Mode 3 — adopt and fill all** (recipient view only)
1. Same adoption step with the Mode 3 agreement wording.
2. The signer clicks **one** place (or a single "Apply to all my places" button). A confirmation states: *"Your signature and initial will be applied to all N places on this document that are yours. M places need you to act on them one by one (listed)."* On confirm, one `fill_all_confirmed` event is recorded and N placements are written, each with method `stamped_auto` pointing at that confirmation.
3. Individual-consent places (§1.7) are not filled; the signer is walked to each with "Go to next".
4. The server builds the list of the signer's own places from the canonical document (`data-recipient-identity` as `CanonicalInkComposer` reads it) — it does **not** trust a client-posted list — so a tampered client cannot add or remove places.

## 5. Evidence design

**New table `signature_adoptions` (append-only).** `id, signature_request_id, envelope_id, signer_name, signer_email, signature_image, initial_image, signature_image_hash, initial_image_hash, disclaimer_version, disclaimer_text (full snapshot), mode, ip_address, user_agent, adopted_at, generation, superseded_at, superseded_by_id`. Re-adoption inserts generation+1 and stamps `superseded_*` on the old row; nothing is updated otherwise or deleted (non-negotiable #1; follow `RentalInspectionSignature` supersede pattern).

**Per-place records REUSE `signature_place_generations`** (built by the signature-evidence fix, branch `esign-evidence-generations-2026-10-23`, `ESIGN-CANON.md` §10 — Johan's rule that a re-signature never overwrites the original; it is a bug fix that lands first). That table already holds, per place: `document_id, signature_request_id, place_key, kind, generation, image, image_hash, signer, ip_address, user_agent, signed_at, superseded_at/by`. Modes 2/3 add three nullable columns to it (step 2): `method` (`drawn` — Mode 1 and a deliberate fresh capture; `stamped_click` — Mode 2; `stamped_auto` — Mode 3), `adoption_id` (which adoption's mark was stamped) and `fill_all_event_id` (Mode 3: the single confirmation). Removal or redo of a place = a new generation, the old one superseded. There is **no separate `signature_placements` table.**

**Audit log (existing `signature_audit_log`).** New free-string actions: `signature_adopted` (metadata: adoption id, disclaimer version, both image hashes, generation), `signature_placed` (place_key, kind, method, adoption id), `fill_all_confirmed` (count filled, count excluded and why, place-list hash), `signature_readopted`. Document hash carried as on existing rows.

**Mode 1 documents** already get a generation row per place from the evidence fix (method `drawn`); nothing else about Mode 1 changes.

**Generation rule check, per mode:** adoption rows and placement rows are never updated except to stamp `superseded_*`; the sealed-version chain continues to hold full document history at each hop; the `web_template_data` JSON is still written (the renderer reads it) but is no longer the only evidence. **The web-path JSON overwrite and `section_acceptances` `updateOrCreate` (§2) were ruled a bug against Johan's rule and are fixed first (`ESIGN-CANON.md` §10).**

**Certificate:** `audit-certificate.blade.php` gains, per party in Modes 2/3: the adoption time, agreement version, and a per-place list (place, kind, method, time). Mode 1 certificates unchanged.

## 6. Agreement wording (versioned)

New config `config/esign-adoption.php`: `version` (integer, bumped on any wording change) and the wording per mode. Platform-wide (legal text, not agency-editable — an agency editing legal wording is a risk, not a setting). Each adoption stores the full text it was shown, so changing wording later never rewrites history.
- **v1 Mode 2 (APPROVED, Johan's words):** "By signing here you agree this is your signature and initial and that it will be applied wherever you click to sign or initial on this document."
- **v1 Mode 3 (APPROVED by Johan as drafted, 23 Oct 2026):** "By signing here you agree this is your signature and initial and that it will be applied to every signature and initial place on this document that is yours, without you clicking each one."

## 10. Risks

- **Legal / doctrine (Mode 3).** Reverses a settled, shipped decision ("recipients initial each page individually for legal informed-consent reasons"). Mitigated by the explicit counted confirmation, the stored agreement, the excluded individual-consent places and the audit — and Johan accepted it as an agency choice (ruling 1, §11); it is arguably still a conveyancer's question. ECTA §13(1) still applies independently: e-sign eligibility is the template's setting and is untouched by this mode.
- **Duplicated JS.** The recipient and agent views carry separate copies of the capture/apply-all code; the partial keeps the new logic in one place, but the 4,000-line recipient view is fragile (the pipeline gate and its tests exist because of past regressions).
- **Web-path evidence gap.** Web/CDS documents store no per-place time/IP today; the new tables fill that gap for Modes 2/3 only. A signer in Mode 1 on the same document type still has the old, thinner evidence.
- **Client-gated counts.** The server today does not know how many places a document has; Mode 3 needs a server-derived place list, which depends on the canonical document carrying reliable `data-recipient-identity` on every place. Documents that predate that stamping, or odd templates, need a graceful "this document cannot be filled automatically — sign each place" fallback to Mode 2 behaviour.
- **Network.** Per-click placement writes can fail on a phone; the client queues and the completion reconcile catches any gap rather than losing a click.
- **Pack initials.** Initials restart per member document and are skipped on each document's last page; counts and keys must follow that rule exactly or the count shown in Mode 3 will be wrong.
- **Untested paths.** Proxy signing and witness-as-recipient have no real data on QA1.
- **Moving target.** QA1 advanced (`fb9ab8d28` → `dd0047de7`) while this was being written; re-verify every cited file and line before building.

## 11. Rulings (Johan, 23 Oct 2026 14:52 — all approved)

1. **Mode 3 is accepted as an agency choice**, with the safeguards: a confirmation that states how many places will be filled, the agreement stored with the signer, and individual-consent places (condition / amendment / "every co-tenant" / disclosure-grid amendment initials) always done one by one.
2. **Agreement wording approved as drafted** (§6) — Mode 2: "By signing here you agree this is your signature and initial and that it will be applied wherever you click to sign or initial on this document." Mode 3: "By signing here you agree this is your signature and initial and that it will be applied to every signature and initial place on this document that is yours, without you clicking each one."
3. **The modes apply to recipients signing by link only.** Agents keep their saved signature (PIN) and apply-to-all.
4. **Adopt once per pack (envelope)**, not once per document.
5. **The setting appears in the new-agency setup wizard once the feature is built and working** (build step 7).

6. **Two choices, one document (Johan, 26 Oct 2026, after testing on QA1).** Options 2 and 3 live on the same document: the agency setting becomes (a) "Sign and initial each place" (default) or (b) "Adopt once" — adopt signature + initial, then click each place AND/OR press "Apply to all my places" (same server rules as before: only the signer's own places, consent/condition/change/disclosure places never auto-filled, per-place audit rows, generations kept). Existing agencies on either old adoption mode map to (b). Adoption wording kept. A click or Apply-to-all with no adoption opens the adopt popup then applies it; apply-to-all is refused client- and server-side until BOTH marks exist. The document header becomes sticky and carries the model, progress, actions and blocker (§1.4). The Setup Wizard and E-Sign Settings screens show the two choices.

*Also decided (no ask):* the setting snapshots onto each document at send; Mode 1 documents are not changed by this work; wording is platform text, not agency-editable; the setting is a separate "How my signers sign" page. The web-path overwrite is a bug fixed first (not a decision): `ESIGN-CANON.md` §10.

## 13. Recipient signing page — first-time signer flow

> Added 26 Oct 2026 (written before the code). Johan reviewed the QA1 recipient page and rejected it for a first-time signer ("we are not going to code like grade 1 kids"); the conductor approved this screen on Johan's behalf. **It replaces the look and wording of §1.4 / §12's header, left column and floating bar. Every server rule stays exactly as built: the saved signature/initial is the only thing ever stamped, a click or "Sign everything" with no saved signature is refused 422 `adoption_required`, one audit row and one generation per place, consent/condition/change/disclosure places are never auto-filled.** The document itself is unchanged.

**What was wrong (verified in a browser):** three counters disagreed ("0 of 2 places", "3 left", "3 items remaining"); the left column said "initial each page / sign at the bottom" while the header said "adopt once"; jargon (adopt, marks, places); "Change my marks" before any exist; "Apply to all" offered before a signature exists; yellow fields gave no clue whether the signer types or CoreX fills them; the Amendments panel was empty furniture; the floating black bar duplicated the header.

### 13.1 Words (one voice)
- Always **"signature and initials"** (a signer may have several initials). Never *adopt, marks, places, stamp* anywhere a signer can read — buttons, headings, status lines, toasts, errors, dialogs.
- Buttons are exactly **Start · Next · Sign everything · Finish** (plus Cancel in dialogs). "Change my signature" appears only after a signature exists.
- Help is one line: **"Questions? Call <agent name> <phone>."**

### 13.2 The sticky header (nothing else in it)
1. Document name · **"Signing as <name> (<role>)"**.
2. **ONE status line:** `Step N of 3: <what to do now>` followed by the single progress figure `x of N done`. In a pack, step 3 reads `Step 3 of 3: Sign — Document 1 of 2 — <name>`.
3. **ONE primary button** for the next action (Start, then Next) beside **Finish** (disabled until everything is done; hovering says exactly what is missing). In step 3 of an adopt-once agency a second outline button, **Sign everything**, sits beside Next (same size, aligned).
4. The blocker is one small line under the status: the single thing missing, with a **Go there** link. Nothing else.
5. The floating black bottom bar is removed.

### 13.3 The three steps (same for a single document and a pack; same on a phone)
| Step | Adopt-once agency | Sign-each agency |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Add your signature and initials** | Start opens the capture (today's popup, retitled "Add your signature and initials"; the agreement sentence is unchanged). Done when a signature AND initials are saved. | Reads **"How you'll sign"**: one line "You'll sign each spot by hand as you go" and a **Start** button. Done when pressed (or the signer has already started). |
| **2 — Check your details** | Every field CoreX could fill is already filled and looks read-only (plain, no yellow). Fields the signer must type are yellow, say **"Type here"** and are listed first (in the left column) with the count "2 details to fill". **Next** goes field to field; with none left, Next confirms the details. A document with nothing for this signer to check skips the step. | Same. |
| **3 — Sign** | **Next** walks to the next spot (signature, initials, then the one-by-one items) and highlights it; pressing **Next** again, or clicking the spot, puts the saved signature/initials there. **Sign everything** = today's apply-to-all with its approved confirmation (count per document in a pack). | **Next** walks to the next spot and, pressed again (or clicking it), opens the pad to sign by hand. No Sign everything. |

The step is *derived* from state (never stored): step 1 until a signature exists (or Start is pressed); step 2 while a detail is empty or not yet confirmed; step 3 after. A signer who clicks a signature spot early is not blocked: with no saved signature the capture popup opens first, then the click is applied (unchanged §1.3).

**Always one-by-one, explained in one line when reached** (the blocker line names it): consent ("Tick the box to say you've read and agree, then press Finish"), a condition ("This condition needs your initials — click it"), a change to the wording ("Initial this change — click it"), a disclosure answer ("This is your own answer — it can't be filled in for you").

### 13.4 One counter
`x of N done` is the page's existing single source (`signedCount` / `totalRequired`, which count details, signature and initials spots, disclosure answers, condition initials, change initials and consent together). The header, the left column and the Finish tooltip all read it; there is no other count anywhere.

### 13.5 The left column (desktop; hidden on a phone, where the header carries the same information)
The three steps, one sentence each, the live one lit, finished ones ticked. Step 1 shows "Change my signature" once one exists (adopt-once). Step 2 lists the details to type (each a link). A one-line "Questions? Call …". The five-paragraph "How to sign" essay and the separate "Signing as" block are deleted.

### 13.6 Amendments
The panel is closed by default behind a small link **"Need to change the wording?"** above the document; it opens today's panel unchanged. It opens by itself when the document already carries wording changes or an amendment is created.

### 13.7 Fields
A recipient-editable field that already has a value is shown plain (it can still be corrected by clicking it). An empty one is yellow with placeholder **"Type here"** (its label stays on the page and in the hover text).

### 13.8 Tests
Step progression; single counter; no jargon in the rendered page; sign-each vs adopt-once headers and buttons; pack status line; Finish tooltip; phone render; floating bar and essay gone; amendments closed by default; saved-signature-only rules unchanged (existing suites).
